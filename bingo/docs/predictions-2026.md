# The 200 predictions for The Game Awards 2026

Reviewed November 26, 2026. The show is on December 10.

This is a curated bingo pool, not a list of confirmed announcements. Research checks the nominees, existence of named projects, and current development context. **Every winner, trailer, update, platform announcement, and on-stage moment in the pool is a prediction.** A source link supports the background, not the predicted TGA appearance.

The pool contains 40 award picks checked against official category pages, 70 predictions involving researched games and studios, and 90 original genre, trailer, and ceremony predictions. The latter are creative watch-party prompts, not news claims or rumors. The indie selection favors roguelikes, deckbuilders, auto-battlers, exploration, unusual mechanics, and smaller teams.

The generator uses all 200 entries, preserves filled squares, and avoids selecting two competing winners for the same category or two reveal predictions from the same project group. You can still type any prediction yourself.

## What the research changed

- At Fate’s End already has a demo and a 2027 window. Its predictions ask for a specific date or fresh footage.
- BALL x PIT’s Risen Ballbylon is already out. Its predictions ask for a show appearance or subsequent content.
- Mina the Hollower’s post-launch plans include randomizer and speedrun work. The pool asks whether that work gets a show announcement.
- Slay the Spire 2 is in active development. The pool predicts its next update or a 1.0 window, not its initial reveal.
- Final Fantasy VII Revelation has already been named. Its predictions concern new footage or a summon.
- Naughty Dog said Intergalactic will be quiet for the rest of 2026, so it was left out of the reveal pool. [Developer statement](https://www.naughtydog.com/blog/neil-druckmann-closes-the-last-of-us-day?pubDate=20260929).
- Slotbound and a new Supergiant tease are deliberate long shots; neither is presented as booked for the show.
